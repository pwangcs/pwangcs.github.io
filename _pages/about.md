I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Computational Imaging and Machine Vision at Westlake University. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology through a joint program between Zhejiang University and Westlake University in 2025. My doctoral thesis focused on deep learning for compressive imaging inverse problems.

My research interests include **computational imaging and photography**, **computer vision**, and **image and video processing**. I work on learning-based reconstruction for inverse problems, snapshot compressive imaging, and spectral and polarization imaging.

My publications cover hyperspectral and polarization imaging, video snapshot compressive imaging, single-pixel imaging, and deep reconstruction methods. See the [publication list](/publications/), find my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/pwangcs), or browse my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WCsIUToAAAAJ&hl=en).
