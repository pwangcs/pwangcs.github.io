## Snapshot compressive imaging

Developing reconstruction methods for snapshot compressive imaging, including video compressive imaging and coded-aperture systems. Related work includes video snapshot compressive imaging, deep optics, and high-speed imaging.

## Spectral and polarization imaging

Learning-based methods for hyperspectral image reconstruction, dual-camera compressive spectral imaging, and color polarization image demosaicking, including joint reconstruction and downstream vision tasks.

## Inverse problems and computational photography

Designing efficient reconstruction networks for ill-posed imaging problems, including algorithm unrolling, single-pixel imaging, compressive sensing, and deep reconstruction methods.
